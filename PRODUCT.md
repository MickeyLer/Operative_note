## Platform

web

## Users

Surgeons and surgical staff at Khon Kaen Hospital (KKH) creating and printing standardized operative notes for hepatobiliary and laparoscopic surgeries.

## Product Purpose

The KKH Digital Op Note system allows surgeons to quickly generate, edit, and print standardized, clean, and professional surgical records. It aims to reduce manual reporting times while increasing note precision and compliance.

## Positioning

Providing highly specialized surgical templates (such as Hepatectomy, Whipple, and Laparoscopic LAR) that feature structured procedural checklists, detailed anatomical findings (e.g., port placements, lymph node selections, and vascular variations), and clean, print-ready layouts.

## Operating Context

- Operative and clinical documentation workflows at Khon Kaen Hospital.
- Post-operative records management where notes must be printed and filed in physical charts.
- Fast-paced environments where standard templates and templates created by users minimize repetitive typing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Templates & Presets:** Standard presets for open/laparoscopic hepatectomies, Whipple procedures, Lap LAR, and Lap Chole, along with custom user-defined templates.
- **Dynamic Checklists:** Step-by-step checkboxes corresponding to typical surgical steps for each procedure.
- **Detailed Findings:** Structured entry for intraoperative findings such as ICG fluorescence, pancreatic duct size, lymph node selection, vascular variations, adhesions, and metastasis.
- **Interactive Mapping:** Interactive tool for mapping laparoscopic port sizes and positions.
- **Supabase Integration:** Real-time data storage for templates and records.
- **Print Layouts:** Highly optimized styles for clean paper prints.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** KKH Digital Op Note (Khon Kaen Hospital Operative Note System).
- **Tone:** Professional, clinical, precise, and highly reliable.

## Evidence on Hand

- High-fidelity React components in [`OperativeForm.tsx`](file:///d:/react/operative_note/src/components/OperativeForm.tsx) mapping the required data schemas, presets, and validation logic.

## Product Principles

1. **Precision & Speed:** Reduce documentation overhead with templates while maintaining surgical detail.
2. **Clinical Utility:** Focus on structured clinical data that maps directly to surgical decisions and anatomical variations.
3. **Print-First Design:** Ensure final output translates flawlessly to physical paper records for hospital archiving.
